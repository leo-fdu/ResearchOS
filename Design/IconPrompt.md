# Research OS icon

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Final artwork: `ResearchOS-Icon.png`.

Prompt: A finished macOS app icon on a transparent canvas. A midnight navy enamel rounded tile, centered ivory and ice-blue stacked research cards forming an angular open-book prism, and a single cyan point where the cards meet. Restrained depth, strong silhouette at small sizes, generous negative space. No lettering, atoms, orbital lines, magnifying glass, purple gradient or generic sparkle. Straight-on view, no scene or mockup.

`MakeIcon.swift` only resamples this artwork to standard icon sizes. It does not regenerate or redraw the design.
